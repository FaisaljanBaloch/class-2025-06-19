# Analysis on Economic Connectedness in Different US Counties
Welcome to the *Economic Connectedness Analysis project!* This repository contains an in-depth analysis of economic connectedness across various counties in the United States. The project leverages publicly available data to explore how economic ties and social mobility vary geographically, and presents findings in an accessible manner.

## Project Overview
**Economic connectedness** refers to the degree to which individuals from different socioeconomic backgrounds interact and form relationships. Understanding these connections is crucial because they have been shown to impact economic mobility and community development.
This project aims to:
- Analyze datasets related to economic connectedness at the county level.
- Visualize patterns and disparities across the US.
- Provide insights into factors that may influence economic connectedness and, consequently, economic opportunity.

## Features
- **Data Analysis**: Clean and process datasets related to economic connectedness.
- **Visualization**: Generate maps, charts, and tables to communicate findings.
- **Interpretation**: Discuss the implications of observed patterns on social mobility and economic development.
